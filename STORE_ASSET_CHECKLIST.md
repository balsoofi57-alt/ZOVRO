# ZOVRO Store Asset Checklist

App name: ZOVRO
Version: 1.0.0
Bundle/App ID: com.zovro.app
Approved support email: support@zovro.net

## Apple App Store assets
Prepare final screenshots from the signed/TestFlight build, not mockups that show features absent from the submitted build.

Recommended screenshot story:
1. Home / service discovery
2. Customer request creation
3. Nearby provider discovery
4. Provider job acceptance
5. Job status tracking
6. Messaging
7. Ratings / provider reputation
8. Profile, privacy, support and account deletion

Also prepare:
- App icon in required App Store source size/format
- Optional promotional artwork only after final brand review
- Subtitle and promotional text if used
- Privacy Policy URL
- Support URL
- Marketing URL if a final public website is selected

## Google Play assets
Prepare:
- Final high-resolution app icon
- Feature graphic
- Phone screenshots from the signed release candidate
- Short description
- Full description
- Privacy Policy URL
- Support contact email: support@zovro.net

Screenshot story should match the Apple sequence where possible for brand consistency.

## Screenshot rules
- Use real production-like data with no real customer personal information.
- Do not show private phone numbers, emails, exact home addresses, API keys, test credentials, or internal tokens.
- Clearly show ZOVRO branding.
- Do not imply that SOS replaces 911.
- Do not show unsupported payment, tracking, or verification claims.
- Keep status bar/time/battery presentation clean and consistent.

## Brand consistency
- App name: ZOVRO
- Bundle/App ID: com.zovro.app
- Core message: local pros for roadside, auto, home, moving and urgent service requests
- Safety message: ZOVRO urgent/SOS requests are marketplace assistance and do not replace emergency services

## Final validation
Before upload, compare every screenshot and store claim against the submitted binary. Remove any asset that promises functionality not enabled in the release build. Verify support@zovro.net can receive and respond to a test support inquiry before submission.
