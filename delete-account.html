Delete Your ZOVRO Account & Data

ZOVRO users can request account deletion directly from the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to ZOVRO.
  2. Open Profile & Security.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request when prompted.

If you cannot access your account

Contact ZOVRO Support at support@zovro.work and request account deletion. Include the phone number or email associated with the account so we can verify the request.

What is deleted

Account identifiers and active profile data are removed or de-identified from active use. Open service requests are cancelled or detached as appropriate, and active session, notification, and location records associated with the account are removed from active use.

What may be retained

Some completed transaction, fraud-prevention, dispute, security, tax, accounting, or legal records may be retained when reasonably necessary or required by law. Retained records are limited to the purposes that require retention and are not kept as an active user profile.

For privacy questions, contact support@zovro.work.
