Support

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Find help for your ZOVRO account, service requests and safety concerns.

Account help

Use Profile to review your account, notifications, provider details or account deletion options.

Service request help

Open Requests or Messages to review an active job, contact the assigned provider and follow job status.

Safety

If you are in immediate danger or need police, fire or medical assistance, call 911. ZOVRO roadside/SOS requests do not replace emergency services.

Provider verification

Providers should keep license and qualification information accurate. Verification status does not replace any legal license, permit or insurance requirement.

Contact support

A monitored public support email and final support contact details must be published before commercial launch. Until then, this page documents the support path for release preparation and store review.
