ZOVRO Support

Account & app help

For account access, service-request, provider, messaging, location, or account-deletion issues, use the support option provided inside the ZOVRO app or the official contact channel published with the store listing.

Safety

ZOVRO is not an emergency-response service. For immediate danger, medical emergencies, fire, or crimes in progress, contact 911.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account from the Profile section of the ZOVRO app.

Technical status

The ZOVRO production service includes automated health and readiness checks. If the app reports that the API is offline, retry after confirming your internet connection.
