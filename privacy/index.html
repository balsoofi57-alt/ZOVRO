ZOVRO Privacy Policy

Effective: September 16, 2026

ZOVRO LLC (“ZOVRO,” “we,” “us”) provides a marketplace that connects customers with independent service professionals. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information.

Information we collect

We may collect account and contact information such as name, email address, mobile phone number, service-provider profile information, service requests, approximate or precise location when permitted, transaction information, device information, and communications with ZOVRO.

How we use information

We use information to operate and secure ZOVRO, match customers with service professionals, process and support service requests, provide account and service notifications, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with law, and improve our services.

SMS messaging

Service professionals may separately opt in to receive SMS messages from ZOVRO about new job opportunities, job status updates, and service notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent data, will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers that help us deliver requested communications and operate ZOVRO, subject to appropriate protections.

Choices

You may decline SMS consent. If you opt in, you can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP. You may contact ZOVRO for privacy or account questions.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information and retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here and applicable legal obligations.

Provider identity verification and government documents

Service professionals and business representatives must verify their legal name, identity and age (18+) using a supported government-issued photo ID or passport. When you choose the secure identity-check flow, Stripe Identity acts as our verification service provider and collects the document images and, where requested, a selfie through its hosted verification process. ZOVRO does not collect or store the original document images, passport or ID numbers in its own application database through this flow. ZOVRO receives the verification status and verified name and birth-date details for age and name checks, and keeps only encrypted legal-name information, non-reversible name comparison data, an age-eligibility result, status, provider reference and timestamps needed for verification and fraud prevention. Such information is not shown to customers. You can contact support to ask about verification records and rights available under applicable privacy law. The provider’s privacy practices and retention may also apply to documents processed by it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ZOVRO LLC at contact@zovro.work.
