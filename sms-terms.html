SMS Messaging Terms

ZOVRO may send transactional text messages to users who provide a mobile number and expressly consent to receive SMS notifications. Messages may include account notices, service-request alerts, job availability, assignment confirmations, status updates, and safety-related service communications.

Consent

Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchase. Users must affirmatively opt in before ZOVRO sends recurring automated text messages. ZOVRO does not use purchased or third-party lead lists for SMS enrollment.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on account activity and service requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for help. After an opt-out request, ZOVRO may send a final confirmation message. Users can opt in again through an authorized ZOVRO consent flow.

Provider job alerts

Eligible service providers may receive a text when a new job is available. When instructed by the message, a provider may reply 1 or APPROVE to request acceptance. Acceptance is confirmed only after ZOVRO verifies that the job remains available and the provider is eligible and available. Declining or ignoring an offer does not assign the job.

Privacy

Mobile information and SMS consent records are used to operate ZOVRO messaging and are handled according to the ZOVRO Privacy Policy. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Support

For assistance, use the ZOVRO Support page.
