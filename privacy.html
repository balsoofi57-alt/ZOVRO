ZOVRO Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026

ZOVRO connects customers with local service providers. The service may process account information, contact details, service requests, messages, ratings, approximate or precise location when you choose to share it, and security/session information needed to operate the service.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, match customers with nearby providers, dispatch urgent requests, support job communication and status updates, prevent abuse, and operate and improve ZOVRO.

Location

Location is requested only for location-based features such as nearby providers, dispatch, and job tracking. You can deny device location permission, although some nearby-service features may then be unavailable.

Sharing

ZOVRO does not intentionally sell personal information. Information needed to complete a service request may be shared between the customer and the provider assigned to that request. Provider verification and account information are handled separately from public profile information.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from the ZOVRO app. The service is designed to remove or de-identify account information while retaining limited operational records when needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

Security

ZOVRO uses authenticated sessions, access controls, rate limits, production secrets, secure transport on hosted services, and other safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

ZOVRO is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as ZOVRO adds production services such as payments, push notifications, maps, or additional identity verification. Material changes will be reflected in the app or this page.
